Recommendations
Home → Ship
H01 Premium Value + H02 Money Covered (A/B)

H01 for users who engaged with health/spending; H02 for users who saw bills/buffer alerts. Both use dark premium cue in feed without dominating. H01 tested as default — strongest “continuous intelligence” framing, compact chart cue at 15% area, clear benefit triad.

Sign-up → Ship
S10 Value + Transparency (default) + S06 fallback

S10 makes price, trial, advisory fee, and waiver visually separate — highest trust at payment moment. S06 as alternate for more aspirational hero. Both satisfy: value → price → trial → disclosures.

Assumptions — $3/month, $36/year, 17% savings, 30-day trial, cancel anytime, 0.25% advisory quarterly in arrears, $1K waiver. Kept configurable: strings/currency are插槽, not hardcoded constants.
Product logic to confirm — Annual price & savings %, whether $1K waiver auto-applies vs needs disclosure, disclosure link targets (Terms/Privacy/Disclosures), offer-code & restore-purchase placement on iOS, analytics for plan pre-selection.
Writing — Outcome-oriented, no “unlock/supercharge/level up”. All headlines answer “Why does this matter to me?” Advisory fee never combined with subscription.
